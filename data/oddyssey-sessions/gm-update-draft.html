What’s New on the Dashboard

1 · Ticket Groups section

On both Manor and Noir summaries, there’s a new Ticket Groups table that pulls actual numbers straight from Ticketure’s per-group Summary Report. Reserved, Redeemed, and Gross (actual) — not the old list-price estimate. Third-party rows get a purple tag so they’re easy to spot.

2 · Channel Mix

A dedicated breakdown showing direct box-office vs third-party reseller split — stacked bar, three headline stats (Direct Gross, Third-Party Gross, Net to Oddyssey), plus the per-line commission detail. Commission rate is parsed automatically from the group name (e.g., “Third Party Sales - 20% Commission”).

3 · Nightly Recap Emails

The Channel Mix block also goes out in the automated recap emails whenever a night has third-party activity. Quiet nights look the same as before — no noise when there’s nothing to report.
