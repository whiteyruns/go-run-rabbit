WHAT IS FEED THE BLOCK?

Feed the Block transforms a single city block in Downtown Las Vegas' Fremont East into an unparalleled street festival experience. Imagine a block party amplified — a neon-drenched celebration where tourists, locals, and industry insiders converge. Flanked by the legendary Fremont Street Experience and the historic El Cortez Hotel, Feed the Block offers a day and night of free revelry.
