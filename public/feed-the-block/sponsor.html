WHY FEED THE BLOCK?

Feed the Block is the only recurring, large-scale street festival on Fremont East — a block-long cultural takeover that merges live music, nightlife, culinary experiences, and brand activations into a single high-energy night. With a young, diverse, high-spending audience and deep integration across seven unique venues, sponsors don't just get impressions — they get moments.

→ 15,000+ attendees per event across 7 unique venues
→ Ages 21-44, 54% Hispanic/Latino, strong female skew
→ 65% dwell time over 45 minutes
→ 10,000+ casino crossover per event
→ 125K+ social followers, 2.45M video views
→ 296.6M earned media impressions — CBS, NBC, FOX5, LVRJ
→ On-site activations: pop-ups, sampling, branded moments, VIP
→ Content inclusion in $25K-$30K/event professional production
